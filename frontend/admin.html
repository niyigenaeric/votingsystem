ELECTION CONTROL

Manage each position

Schedules use Rwanda local time (Africa/Kigali). Use Manage candidates on a position to add, edit, or delete its candidates before voting starts. Closed positions can be archived to remove them from the active list and voter ballots; their records are retained. Voting does not start automatically: an administrator opens a position after its scheduled start. Each position closes automatically at its scheduled end, or may be closed earlier.
