ELECTION CONTROL

Manage each position

Schedules use Rwanda local time (Africa/Kigali). Use Manage position to edit position details before voting starts. If the scheduled start passes before voting is opened, you can reschedule the unopened election; existing candidates remain unchanged. Closed positions can be archived manually to remove them from the active list and voter ballots; their records are retained in Archived elections. Voting does not start automatically: an administrator opens a position after its scheduled start. Each position closes automatically at its scheduled end, or may be closed earlier.
